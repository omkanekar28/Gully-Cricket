How to play

Batting. Tap the screen as the ball reaches you: left, middle or right aims leg side, straight or off side, and the top half lofts it. Block, Step out and the ◀ ▶ buttons (hold to move across the crease) are below. On a keyboard, hold arrows to aim (two together for diagonals), then press S for a ground shot, D for a lofted shot or A to block. Shift + Left/Right moves you across the crease, Shift + Up steps down the pitch and Shift + Down steps back. You can move up to about 1 m either side and 1.5 m down the pitch, before or during the delivery, and you walk back to the crease after each ball. Moving away from the stumps exposes them, and stepping out turns a bouncing ball into a half-volley that’s easier to hit straight, at your own risk.

Bowling. All bowling is underarm. Drag on the pitch or use the arrow keys to place the landing marker, pick Flat (about 50 km/h), Lob (about 35 km/h) or Spinner (about 40 km/h) with the buttons or 1, 2, 3, then press Space, Enter or Bowl to start the walk-up. Press again (or Release) while the needle is in the green.

Extras. A full toss that reaches the batter is a no-ball: +1 and a free hit, on which the batter can’t be out. A ball that bounces three or more times is a no-ball: +1. A wide is +1 and bowled again. You can’t be out off a no-ball either.

Scoring. Any bat touch, even a block or edge, scores 1. Bounce into a side wall before the centre line: 1. After the centre line: 2. One bounce and then out of the court: 4. A straight hit that clears the far railing on the full: 6. A ball that rolls (bounces more than once) to the far railing scores 2.

Out. Hitting any wall, railing or the gates on the full, going over a side wall on the full, caught or bowled. There’s one batter at a time and no running. Three wickets end the innings.

Camera and info. Camera (or C) cycles Default, Bowling view, Side-on, High, Close and Manual. In Manual, drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, and use I, J, K, L to pan. Lock view freezes the camera for the rest of the game; tap Unlock camera, or start a new game from this menu, to free it. Info (or H) hides the hints and labels.
