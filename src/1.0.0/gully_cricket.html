📖 HOW TO PLAY & GULLY RULES

Office Gully Cricket combines street cricket rules with 3D arcade timing!

🏏 BATTING & DIRECTIONAL ARROWS

  • ⏳ 10s Prep Window: Before each delivery, you have 10 seconds to move with WASD and tune Shot Power (20%-100%). Press [Space / Enter] or click Ready / Bowl Now when prepared!
  • ▲ Straight Drive: Drive straight down the pitch towards the 4-run Front Wall.
  • ▲+◄ / ▲+► Diagonals: Cover Drive (Off) or Mid-Wicket On-Drive into front gaps.
  • ◄ / ► Side Touches: Safe deflection cuts along the pavers towards side walls.
  • ▼ Safe Block (▼ + S/D): Dead-bat defensive block straight into the pavers. Safe 1 run & recovers -25% Fatigue!
  • ⚠️ Tactical Trap: If the bowler bowls a Lofted delivery and a fielder is stationed close-by (<5.5m), the Safe Block pops up softly for an easy catch = OUT!

🏟️ WALL BOUNDARIES & RUNS

  • 🔴 Front Wall: 4 Runs (Must bounce on ground first!)
  • 🔵 Far Side Walls: 2 Runs (Must bounce on ground first!)
  • 🟢 Close Side Walls: 1 Run (Must bounce on ground first!)
  • 🚫 Direct Wall Hit: Hitting ANY wall directly without a bounce = OUT!
  • 🛡️ Safe Open Gap: 1 Run!
  • ⚠️ Over the Wall on the fly: OUT! (Lost ball penalty).

⚾ UNDERARM BOWLING & EXTRAS

  • Underarm Only: Standing delivery, strictly 1 bounce.
  • Wides: Outside wide lines = +1 run and rebowl.
  • No-Ball: Knee-height above stumps with 0 bounces = +1 run and rebowl.
  • Delivery Types: Flat & Lofted (Safe) vs Leg-Spin & Off-Spin (High Risk, Sharp Turn).

⚠️ THE 3-SECOND "CALL DEAD" RULE (BATTERS ONLY)

  • Double-Bounce Ball: If the ball bounces more than once before reaching the crease, the batsman can still hit it!
  • Batters Only: The Call Dead privilege belongs exclusively to the batting team. The bowling team cannot call dead-ball.
  • 3s Window: When batting, you have 3 seconds to press [D] or click "CALL DEAD" to cancel the delivery and rebowl!
  • Strategy: If you hit a 4, let the timer expire to keep the runs! If you get out or play a dot, call Dead Ball!

🧤 FIELDING & CATCHES

  • Direct Catch: Caught in the air = OUT!
  • One-Drop Catch: Caught on 1 bounce = OUT! (Classic Gully One-Tip One-Hand).
  • Fielding Skill (0-10): Low-skill fielders drop catches and react slower.
  • Preset Formations: Choose Balanced, Wall Trap, or Deep Defense before each over.

🔥 FATIGUE & RAGE MECHANIC

  • Bowler Fatigue: Bowling risky spin balls builds fatigue, leading to accidental Wides, above-knee No-Balls, or slow Dead-Balls.
  • Batter Fatigue: Aggressive power drives (>70% power) tire the batter (+20% fatigue). High fatigue causes:
    • Mistimed Aerial Slices: The ball balloons into the air, triggering direct wall hit outs, catches, or flying over the wall!
    • Slower Bat Swing: Delayed downswing increases clean bowled risk.
  • Recovery: Play the Safe Block (▼ or ▼+S/D) to recover -25% fatigue!
  • Rage Mode: Amrit, Darren, Om, and Shubham enter Rage after consecutive failures: extreme rocket power or instant disaster!
